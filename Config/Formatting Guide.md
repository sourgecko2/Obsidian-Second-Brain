## Text Formatting

**Bold text** - use `**text**` or `__text__`

_Italic text_ - use `*text*` or `_text_`

_**Bold and italic**_ - use `***text***` or `___text___`

~~Strikethrough~~ - use `~~text~~`

==Highlighted text== - use `==text==`
