One job per app, decided by **what makes me act on the thing** — not by what topic it belongs to.

> [!warning] If it has to happen at a moment:
>  **Apple Reminders**, with a date and time. Deadlines, calls, bins, anything where the cost of forgetting is real. Reminders is the only system here that can reach my watch and lock screen, so anything time-bound that lives anywhere else is effectively unowned.

> [!note] If it takes up a block of time:
>  **Apple Calendar**, in the Tasks calendar. Study blocks, gym, shifts, appointments. If it needs an hour of my day, it needs an hour on the calendar.

> [!IMPORTANT] If I'll do it when I next sit down:
>  **Obsidian**, as a checkbox in the note it belongs to. Project work, goals, ideas, someday. No dates required and none implied.
## Where things live in Obsidian
- **`Captures`** — inbox. Everything from the share sheet lands here, unsorted. It gets emptied in the morning as part of the daily note. 
- **`Main Goals`** — the horizon list, dailies through end of year. Reviewed weekly, not daily.
- **Daily notes** — today only. Goals in the morning, diary at night.
- **`Notes/`** — anything I'll want to find again.
## Review cadence
- **Every morning** — daily note: set goals, drain `Captures`.
- **Every evening** — diary entry.
- **Weekly** — read `Main Goals`, move anything date-bound into Reminders, archive what's done.
